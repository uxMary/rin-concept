Homecoming: a new RIN logo animation

The current animation (and every earlier reveal) starts at the centre and pushes outward. This one runs the other way. The core lands first, then the six arrows fly in from outside along their own direction, dock clockwise. Everything starts black and turns green as each piece matches into place. Work goes out, results come home to one place.

Loader (relay)

For “RIN is working”. A nudge passes clockwise from arrow to arrow, like a hand-off between departments. The core stays still; it only turns once, during the intro.

Mark-only intro

Beats 1 to 3 only, 1.7 s, black to green. For app launch or the login screen, where the wordmark is already elsewhere.

Small sizes stay still

At 32 px and below the mark doesn’t animate. The motion only reads at about 64 px and up.

How it differs from the current .mov

  • Direction. The .mov is dot → orbit → gather → burst → settle, so the logo explodes out of a point. Homecoming converges: nothing grows from a dot, and no shape morphs.
  • Uses the real pieces. Each of the seven shapes in small.svg moves as itself, along the axis its arrow already points. No stand-in circles.
  • Order means something. Arrows dock clockwise from the top, so the build reads as a sequence. The idle relay reuses that order instead of adding new decoration.
  • Shorter. 2.55 s to the full lockup (1.7 s mark-only), against about 6 s per loop in the .mov. That suits a splash or a video sting.
  • Prototype only. The “rin” letters are cut from Logo.png as a mask, so they can be recoloured. A final export would use the vector wordmark. Reduced-motion settings jump straight to the finished logo.
